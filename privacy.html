Privacy Policy

This policy explains local tool processing, hosting and font requests, advertising cookies, user choices, and how to contact ZenFlow Tools.

Information the tools do not collect

ZenFlow Tools does not ask for an account and does not intentionally collect the text, code, JSON, passwords, passphrases, files, SVG markup, cron expressions, or timer labels entered into a tool. These values are handled by JavaScript in the current browser tab. They are not submitted to a ZenFlow Tools application server.

The site does not request your name, postal address, telephone number, payment details, or account credentials to use the utilities. Do not send secrets or confidential material in a support email or public GitHub issue.

Processing on your device

Tool inputs normally live in browser memory and disappear when the page is refreshed or closed. A tool may use the system clipboard only after you select a copy action. Where a preference uses localStorage, that preference stays in site storage controlled by your browser and can be removed through browser settings. Downloads are created on your device after you request them.

Your browser, operating system, clipboard manager, extensions, or device backup software can independently retain information. Those products are outside this site's control, so use a trusted device and review clipboard history when handling sensitive work.

Hosting, fonts, and technical logs

The hosting provider receives normal web requests and may process an IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamps, and security logs needed to deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts receives a request when the typefaces load and may receive similar connection metadata. Blocking the font request simply causes system fonts to be used.

Google AdSense and cookies

Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads.

You can also learn about interest-based advertising choices at https://www.aboutads.info/. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although doing so may affect advertising preferences or other site behavior. Where applicable law requires a consent choice, the advertising system must honor that choice before using optional storage.

Children, security, and changes

The site is a general-purpose utility collection and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly request personal information from children. No website or device can guarantee absolute security, but keeping tool inputs out of an application database reduces one common exposure path.

This policy may change when site behavior, providers, or legal requirements change. The date at the bottom identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected here rather than hidden in a tool modal.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to wuyung027@gmail.com. Describe the request and the relevant page, but do not include passwords, private code, customer records, or other sensitive content. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide rights concerning personal information held by service providers.

International visitors and data choices

Visitors may reach the site from regions with different privacy rules. Advertising personalization, consent prompts, and available opt-out controls can therefore vary. You can use browser controls to restrict third-party cookies, clear stored site data, or browse with advertising personalization disabled. Requests about provider-held data must be directed to the relevant provider when ZenFlow Tools does not possess that data.

Links to external policies are provided so readers can verify current provider practices. Opening an external link leaves ZenFlow Tools and is governed by that destination. This policy separates ordinary delivery data from the private contents you place inside a tool editor.
