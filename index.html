Make time for what matters

A gentler way to get things done.

01 / Choose one intention

Pick a specific task before you start: outline a page, work through a problem, or read a chapter. Keep a note nearby for ideas that can wait.

02 / Give it 25 minutes

Start a Pomodoro and give that one task your attention. If 25 minutes feels too long, pause when you need to. The timer is a guide, not a rule.

03 / Take a real break

After a focus session, choose a five-minute break. Stand up, stretch, or look away from the screen. After four sessions, try a 15-minute long break.
