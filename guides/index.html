The tools calculate, format, compare, or preview. The guides cover the surrounding judgment: how to test a focus interval, what to remove before pasting code, and how to compare sound with silence. They avoid universal promises and keep examples reproducible.

Start with the guide closest to your current task, then open its linked utility. For critical work, follow the verification steps in your own destination system.

Each guide names the assumptions behind its advice, offers a repeatable observation method, and links back to the exact utility involved. Keep notes from your own environment and revise the method when the evidence points elsewhere.
